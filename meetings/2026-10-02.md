Tshepang: I want to understand what compiler behavior changes don't need t-lang to be approved.

TC: It's a t-types PR... If you look in the RFCs repo, RFC 3254 has a set of delegations that the lang team made to the types team covering matters that affect the languages at the fringes of behavior that are not strictly user-facing or part of the user interface of Rust that users are going to normally run into. Tshepang: Cool. It's nice that it's been written somewhere. Thank you. I've never seen the RFC.
