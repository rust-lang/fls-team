### Pointer terminology and `dangling`

#### [PR 715](https://github.com/rust-lang/fls/pull/715): remove "indirection" and its derivatives

- Pete requested changes on August 7 after finding places where replacing "indirection" with "pointer" could broaden rules beyond the Reference's scope.
- The replies show agreement on the broad direction. Hristian agreed that the FLS needs a `data pointer type` category containing raw pointer and reference types, alongside function pointer types under the broader `pointer type` category. He also agreed that existing pointer-related semantics need to be reexamined and narrowed where they apply only to data pointers.
- There is also general agreement that the `dangling` definition should move toward the Reference's model, including allocation liveness and its zero-sized-pointee exception. Hristian noted, however, that the issue 716 analysis may require provenance to participate in the definition.
- What remains unsettled is the exact definition of the broad pointer category, which existing rules need to use `data pointer type`, whether `dangling` should follow the Reference verbatim or incorporate provenance, and how much of that audit belongs in PR 715 rather than follow-up work.

This is important, but we may be able to work this async in favor of the 1.97 release priority.

Hristian: Yes, the PR needs work.

Pete: This will probably come after 1.97

Hristian: Yes

#### [Issue 716](https://github.com/rust-lang/fls/issues/716): a pointer in a const does not need to be non-dangling

- Hristian's August 12 response confirmed the problem and explored reference validity, raw pointers in final constant values, and provenance to allocations deallocated during const evaluation.
- Mira's August 13 follow-up distinguishes provenance to a dead allocation from a live allocation with an out-of-bounds address and questions whether the Reference's "pointer fragments" wording covers the accepted `wrapping_add` example.
- Decide whether the minimal correction should remove the `non-dangling` requirement now and track final-value provenance liveness and the general definition of `dangling` separately.
- Decide whether issue 716 blocks PR 715 or whether the terminology PR can proceed with deliberately limited scope.
