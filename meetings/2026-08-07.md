TC: We've had some discussions. MiniRust and a-mir-formality are both formalizations. I'm not sure about the degree to which they make sense to replace the normative language in the Reference itself. But for a normative language, what's important is the rule. ??

TC: No.

Pete: There's a [big warning note](https://doc.rust-lang.org/reference/behavior-considered-undefined.html#behavior-considered-undefined) on the "behavior considered undefined" page. At the point where the model is known, the warning goes away?
